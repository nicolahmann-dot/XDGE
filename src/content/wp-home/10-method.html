WHAT TO EXPECT

High challenge. High support.

Your individuality is your distinctive edge.

Your strengths, interests, passions and values are our starting point. We help you develop your own leadership identity around what makes you different.

Built around your next step.

We start with your goals. Your challenges, project and portfolio are shaped around your next opportunity, whether competitive school entry, university admissions or career progression.

Work as a peer alongside experienced professionals.

Throughout your programme, you will be treated as a respected leadership peer, with opportunities to exchange ideas, contribute your perspectives and receive expert feedback on your projects and showcase. Beyond working with your programme leader, you will engage with senior leaders, industry specialists and entrepreneurs through experiences such as company projects, professional visits, boardroom discussions and roundtable events. The nature, frequency and level of industry engagement will depend on your chosen programme. These experiences are embedded throughout your leadership development, helping you build the confidence, mindset and skills to think, act and contribute as a leader every day, not just when an opportunity arises.

Out of your seat. Into the action.

Expect a lively, welcoming environment with practical activities, teamwork, discussion and real challenges. You'll test ideas, solve problems and learn through experience.

Your challenge. Your choice.

Choose your challenge level, project and showcase format. We combine ambitious expectations with the guidance and coaching to help you achieve them.

Different starting points. Shared high standards.

We welcome a wide range of abilities, learning needs and starting levels, including neurodivergent learners. Your starting point does not define your potential - your commitment does.
